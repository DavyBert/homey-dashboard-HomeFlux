HomeFlux gives you a clear live view of the energy flows in and around your home. Solar panels, home batteries, the electricity grid, EV chargers and home consumption come together in one visual dashboard widget, with dynamic energy-flow lines and automatic day/night display.

You choose which Homey devices, capabilities and Logic variables HomeFlux uses. The app runs locally on Homey, can automatically calculate home consumption when no separate consumption meter is available, and offers settings for labels, units, refresh interval and additional dashboard items.
